GLOSSARY

The words you’ll hear at the doctor.

If your doctor speaks English, here is each term in both languages, with a plain explanation.

Stages

MenopausiaMenopause
When you have gone 12 months in a row without a period.
PerimenopausiaPerimenopause
The years before menopause, when hormones rise and fall.
PosmenopausiaPostmenopause
Everything after menopause.
Menopausia quirúrgicaSurgical menopause
Menopause caused by surgery that removes the ovaries.
Menopausia prematuraEarly menopause
Menopause before age 45.

Symptoms

BochornosHot flashes
Sudden heat in the face, neck, or chest, sometimes with sweating.
Sudores nocturnosNight sweats
Hot flashes during sleep that wake you up sweaty.
Mente nubladaBrain fog
Forgetfulness and trouble concentrating.
InsomnioInsomnia
Trouble falling asleep or staying asleep.
PalpitacionesHeart palpitations
Feeling your heart race or pound.
Resequedad vaginalVaginal dryness
Less vaginal moisture, which can cause discomfort or pain.
Dolor en las relacionesPainful sex
Pain during sex. The medical term is dyspareunia.
Deseo sexual bajoLow libido
Less interest in sex.
Regla irregularIrregular periods
Periods that come earlier, later, heavier, or lighter.
Sangrado después de la menopausiaPostmenopausal bleeding
Any bleeding after 12 months without a period. Always tell your doctor.
Dolor de articulacionesJoint pain
Pain or stiffness in knees, hands, shoulders, or other joints.

Hormones & body

EstrógenoEstrogen
The main hormone that drops in menopause.
ProgesteronaProgesterone
A hormone that protects the uterus when you take estrogen.
TestosteronaTestosterone
A hormone women have too; it affects desire and energy.
FSH (hormona foliculoestimulante)FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone)
A hormone sometimes tested, though in perimenopause it changes a lot from day to day.
OvariosOvaries
The organs that make estrogen and progesterone.
Útero (matriz)Uterus (womb)
The organ where your period comes from.
TiroidesThyroid
A gland in the neck. Its problems can look like menopause.
InflamaciónInflammation
The body’s defense reaction. It rises when estrogen drops.

Treatments

Terapia de reemplazo hormonal (TRH)Hormone replacement therapy (HRT)
Treatment that replaces estrogen, and sometimes progesterone.
Terapia hormonalHormone therapy (HT / MHT)
Another name for HRT. Doctors use both.
Estrógeno vaginalVaginal estrogen
A cream, ring, or tablet that works only in the vaginal area.
ParchePatch
Estrogen that sticks to the skin.
DIU hormonalHormonal IUD
A device in the uterus that releases progesterone.
FitoestrógenosPhytoestrogens
Plant substances, like soy and flax, similar to estrogen.
AnticoagulanteBlood thinner
A medication that thins the blood, like warfarin.
Efectos secundariosSide effects
Unwanted reactions to a medication.
RecetaPrescription
A doctor’s order for a medication.

Tests & results

Análisis de sangreBlood test
A blood sample to check hormones, vitamins, or other levels.
Densitometría óseaBone density scan (DEXA)
A scan that measures how strong your bones are.
OsteoporosisOsteoporosis
Weak bones that break more easily.
MamografíaMammogram
An X-ray of the breasts to check for cancer.
PapanicolaouPap smear
A test of the cervix to find changes or cancer.
Presión arterialBlood pressure
The force of blood in your arteries.
ColesterolCholesterol
Fat in the blood. High levels raise heart risk.
TriglicéridosTriglycerides
Another type of fat in the blood.

At the appointment

GinecólogoOB-GYN
A doctor who specializes in women’s health.
Médico de cabeceraPrimary care doctor
Your general doctor.
Especialista certificado en menopausiaCertified menopause practitioner (MSCP)
A clinician with special training in menopause.
Referencia (pase)Referral
When your doctor sends you to a specialist.
IntérpreteInterpreter
A person who translates at your visit. Many U.S. clinics provide one free if you ask.

Useful phrases for your visit

  • Creo que estoy en la perimenopausia.I think I’m in perimenopause.
  • Mi regla ha cambiado.My periods have changed.
  • Tengo bochornos y sudores nocturnos.I’m having hot flashes and night sweats.
  • No estoy durmiendo bien.I’m not sleeping well.
  • Me gustaría hablar de la terapia hormonal.I’d like to talk about hormone therapy.
  • ¿Me puede mandar con un especialista en menopausia?Can you refer me to a menopause specialist?
  • ¿Qué riesgos tiene esto para mí?What are the risks for me?
  • ¿Me lo puede explicar más despacio?Could you explain that more slowly?
  • Necesito un intérprete, por favor.I need an interpreter, please.
